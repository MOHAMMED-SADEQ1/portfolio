Overview

About This Project

The University Student Service System is a comprehensive mobile platform that connects students with their academic life. Powered by a PHP backend and a Flutter mobile app, it centralizes course registration, grade tracking, and academic schedules in one clean experience.

Students receive instant notifications for announcements, deadlines, and new grades, while the system integrates seamlessly with existing university infrastructure. The result is a single trusted place for everything a student needs during the academic year.
